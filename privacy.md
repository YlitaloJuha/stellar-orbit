# Stellar Orbit Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 24, 2026*

Stellar Orbit is a single-player game for Windows, macOS, and Linux. It works offline. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your data. It covers the full game and the demo, on Steam and on itch.io.

## What Stellar Orbit stores on your computer

The game saves a few files in its own user data folder:

- `unlocks.cfg`: best scores, unlocked ships and skins, clearances, and victories
- `settings.cfg`: your video, audio, accessibility, language, and control settings
- `logs/`: the game engine's log files, used to find bugs
- `crash_reports/`: diagnostic reports, only when you choose to create one

Photo mode saves pictures to a `Stellar Orbit` folder in your Pictures folder. When you play through Steam, they are also added to your Steam screenshots.

These files never leave your computer unless you send them yourself, or Steam syncs them (see below).

## Steam features

When you play through Steam, the game uses the Steamworks API from Valve:

- **Achievements and stats** are stored on your Steam account.
- **Leaderboards** get your run score. Steam shows it next to your Steam name.
- **Steam Cloud** syncs `unlocks.cfg` and `settings.cfg` between your computers.

Achievements and leaderboards are in the full game only, not the demo.

Steam handles this data under the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/). The itch.io builds do not include Steam and use none of these features.

## Crash reports

A diagnostic report is a plain text file. It holds:

- the game version and your operating system
- your CPU, GPU, graphics driver, memory, and screen size
- your current run (world, ship, score) and game settings
- recent game events and engine log lines

You create a report in **Settings → Profile → Generate report**. If the game did not shut down cleanly last time, it offers to create one on the next launch. You can say no.

The game never sends a report by itself. It saves the file and shows you where it is. You choose whether to email it to support.

## What Stellar Orbit does not do

- No analytics, crash reporting services, tracking, or advertising.
- No account of its own and no sign-in. Steam features use your existing Steam account.
- No personal information is asked for or collected.
- The only links the game opens are its Steam store page and YouTube channel, in the Steam overlay or your browser.

## Deleting your data

In the game, **Settings → Profile → Erase personnel record** wipes all saved progress.

To remove everything, delete the game's user data folder:

- **Windows:** `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Stellar Orbit`
- **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Stellar Orbit`
- **Linux:** `~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Stellar Orbit`

The demo uses a folder named `Stellar Orbit Demo` in the same place.

## Children

Stellar Orbit does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
